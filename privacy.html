Privacy Policy

Michiel Kustermans provides VK De Pezerik App for free for the players, staff, and supporters of VK De Pezerik. This policy explains what the app stores and who can see it.

Account

You sign in with an email address and password, or with Google or Apple. Those sign-in services and Firebase Authentication store the account identifier, email address, and display name needed to recognize you. The app does not use phone-number sign-in.

Club data

The app stores club information in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so the team can use it together. That includes the club, teams, players, matches, scores, availability, lineups, announcements, training, fines, and membership fees. People in the same club can see the club data their role allows. Managers and admins can edit it. Supporters have a more limited view.

Photos

If you add a match photo, the app uploads that photo to Firebase Storage. People in the club can see photos that are shared there. The photo library permission is only used when you choose a photo.

Calendar

If you add a match to your calendar, that event is written on your device. The app does not upload your calendar.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging stores a device token so the app can send match, result, training, and announcement notifications. You can turn those off in the app settings.

What this app does not do

The app does not sell personal information and does not use it for advertising. It does not ask you to track activity across other companies' apps.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase, for accounts, the club database, photo storage, and push notifications
  • Apple, if you sign in with Apple

Children

The app is for a club, not a service directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.
